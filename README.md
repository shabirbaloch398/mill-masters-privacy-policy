## 1. Introduction

SHABIR INTERACTIVE respects the privacy of players using MILL MASTERS. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using our mobile game.

## 2. Offline Gameplay

MILL MASTERS currently provides offline Nine Men's Morris gameplay, including single-player and local two-player modes.

Players do not need to register, create an account, or sign in to use the game.

## 3. Information Collection

The current version of MILL MASTERS does not require players to provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or location.

Subject to verification of the included software components, the current version does not transmit personal information to SHABIR INTERACTIVE's servers.

## 4.Advertising and Monetization

MILL MASTERS is an offline Nine Men's Morris game developed by SHABIR INTERACTIVE.

We plan to monetize MILL MASTERS through Google AdMob advertisements.

When advertising is enabled, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain information, including:

Device identifiers and advertising identifiers, where available

IP address and approximate location inferred from it

Advertisement interactions and performance information

Device and application information

Diagnostic and fraud-prevention information

This information may be used for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and other purposes described in Google's policies.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

MILL MASTERS does not currently require users to create an account or sign in to play.
